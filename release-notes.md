# Rustym v1.0.2 🎵

**Now Playing learns gestures.** Swipe to change songs, swipe the artwork for volume, lyrics on your terms, a cleaner Settings page — and Rustym now keeps itself up to date. Same universal APK for arm64, armv7, x86 and x86_64 devices (Android 8.0+).

---

## ✨ What's new

### 👆 Now Playing gestures
- **Swipe the card left or right** to play the next or previous song — no need to hit the skip buttons
- **Swipe up or down on the album art** to raise or lower the volume — a live volume pill shows the exact level as you drag

### 🎓 One-time gesture tutorial
- The first time you open Now Playing after updating, a short overlay shows you the two new gestures
- Seen once, remembered forever — it never comes back on later launches

### 📝 Lyrics on your terms
- New **Settings → Lyrics** switch: turn lyrics fetching and display off completely, or back on — the current song loads instantly when you flip it on
- Every source stays the same: Unison, Better Lyrics, KuGou, LRCLIB, NetEase and Lyrics.ovh

### 🧹 Cleaner Settings
- Consistent, tidied switch rows across Appearance and the rest of the page
- Same features, less clutter

### 🔄 Update check, built in
- Rustym **checks for a new version automatically at startup** and offers to open the release page when one exists
- A **"What's new"** dialog greets you once after every update, with the release notes
- The Settings → Updates card now shares the same lookup

---

## 📲 Install
1. Download **Rustym.apk** below
2. Open the file and install it (allow "Install unknown apps" if Android asks)
3. Updating keeps your favourites, history, settings and offline cache — nothing is lost
