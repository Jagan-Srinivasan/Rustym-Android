# Rustym v1.0.6 🎵

**The volume-zero hint now blinks.** One small polish on top of v1.0.5's auto-update feature — everything else is unchanged.

---

## ✨ What's improved

### 🔊 Blinking volume-zero hint
- When you swipe the album art all the way down to **0**, the **"Volume is 0 · swipe up to increase"** pill now **pulses (blinks) between dim and full brightness** so it actually catches your eye instead of sitting quietly
- It keeps blinking **until you raise the volume**, then disappears as usual

---

## ✨ Still new in 1.0.x (if you're jumping several versions)
- 👆 **Swipe the Now Playing card left / right** to change songs
- 🎚️ **Swipe up / down on the album art** to change the volume
- 🤖 **Auto-update & install** — Rustym downloads and stages updates in the background (Settings → Updates)
- 🎓 **One-time gesture tutorial** right after updating — never shown again afterwards
- 📝 **Settings → Lyrics** switch · 🧹 **cleaner Settings page** · 🔄 **startup update check** with a formatted "What's new" dialog

---

## 📲 Install
1. Download **Rustym.apk** below and install it (allow "Install unknown apps" once)
2. Or just let the app do it: if you already have **Auto-update & install** switched on, the update arrives by itself
